What Is The Trybe

Heritage, Worn Every Day

Ndoki Trybe is the section of Ndoki dedicated to street wear — where Akwete motifs move off the runway and onto everyday silhouettes. Like the main house, the Trybe is organised into Menswear, Womenswear and Bespoke, and operates as a substore within the wider Ndoki store.

About Ndoki

A living archive, worn.

At the heart of the brand is Akwete textile, a signature handwoven fabric meticulously crafted by the women of Ndoki land in Eastern Nigeria. Each Akwete textile is more than fabric — it is a living archive, carrying motifs that symbolize identity, history, values and community.

By working directly with local artisans, Ndoki preserves indigenous craftsmanship while creating contemporary designs that honor the past and embrace the future.

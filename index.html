Our Story

About Ndoki

Ndoki is an African sustainable fashion brand rooted in culture, heritage and storytelling. At the heart of the brand is Akwete textile — a signature handwoven fabric meticulously crafted by the women of Ndoki land in Eastern Nigeria.

Each Akwete textile is more than fabric, it is a living archive. Handwoven using techniques passed down through generations, the cloth carries deep cultural meaning, with motifs that symbolize identity, history, values and community. Every pattern tells a story of lineage, resilience, womanhood and tradition.

By working directly with local artisans, Ndoki preserves indigenous craftsmanship while creating contemporary designs that honor the past and embrace the future. Sustainability is not a trend for Ndoki, it is a commitment to ethical production, cultural preservation and empowering women through economic opportunity.

Ndoki stands at the intersection of heritage and modernity, transforming tradition into timeless fashion.
